Tips & Tricks

Learn how to import questions faster using AI

✦ Powered by Google Gemini

Import Questions in Seconds

Instead of typing questions one by one, use Gemini AI to convert any source — PDF, image, or photo — directly into a CSV you can import here.

Method 1 — Soft Copy (PDF or Image File)
You already have a digital file of the questions
1
Open gemini.google.com on your browser
2
Click the attach / paperclip icon and upload your PDF or image file
3
Paste the prompt below (edit the question range first), then send
4
Copy the CSV output from Gemini and go to Import CSV in the sidebar
Method 2 — Hard Copy (Printed Paper)
You only have a physical printed copy of the questions
1
Use your phone's camera — switch to Document Scanner mode (available in most camera apps) for a flattened, high-contrast scan
2
Make sure the photo is clear, well-lit, and all text is readable — blurry photos give bad results
3
Send the photo to Gemini (mobile app or web) and attach the prompt below
4
Copy the CSV output and import it here via Import CSV
Pro tip: Google PhotoScan, Microsoft Lens, or your phone's built-in scanner app can all produce clean, readable document images that Gemini reads perfectly.
The Gemini Prompt
Copy this exactly — just replace [start] and [end] with your question numbers
Convert questions [start] to [end] into a standard CSV format. Use the columns: 'Question', 'Option A', 'Option B', 'Option C', 'Option D', 'Correct Answer'. CRITICAL RULE: Do NOT make a separate column for the question number. Instead, prefix the question number directly inside the 'Question' column text (for example: "31. Which type of pump..."). Wrap every text field in double quotes.
Open Gemini
After Gemini gives you the CSV, click Import CSV in the sidebar, paste the text or upload the saved file, choose your deck, and import!
📋 Quick Reminder — CSV Format

QuizMaster expects a CSV with these exact column headers:

Question Option A Option B Option C Option D Correct Answer

The Correct Answer column should contain just the letter: A, B, C, or D. You can download a sample CSV from Settings → Sample CSV.
